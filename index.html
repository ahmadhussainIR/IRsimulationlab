    ABOUT THE LEARNING MODELS

    Explore with context.

    These synthetic imaging studies demonstrate broad concepts. They are not real patient acquisitions or a complete clinical curriculum.

    How to use the lab

    Choose a procedure, play its sequence, or use the timeline to inspect an individual stage. Try the knowledge check, then follow the clinical reference for context.

    What the animations represent

    AI-generated radiographic backgrounds are combined with animated device and contrast overlays. Wire position, flow, device deployment, tissue sampling, and fluid drainage are deliberately simplified. Motion and timing are illustrative; they do not simulate device physics, real imaging, treatment success, or clinical competence.

    Clinical review

    Created as an educational prototype. It has not undergone independent clinical validation. No medication doses, device sizing, radiation settings, or patient-specific treatment recommendations are provided.

    Clinical reading

    Procedure overviews from RadiologyInfo.org (ACR/RSNA), UCLA Health, CIRSE, and NHS patient-information resources. Consult current professional guidelines and local protocols during supervised training.
